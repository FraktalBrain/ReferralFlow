# ReferralFlow

**A referral management and operational intelligence prototype for therapy practices.**

ReferralFlow explores how therapy practices can centralize referral tracking, improve follow-up coordination, and gain better visibility into their intake pipeline.

The goal is to replace fragmented referral workflows with a more organized, measurable process.

## The Problem

Therapy practices often manage referrals across spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected systems. This can make it difficult to identify stalled referrals, prioritize follow-ups, track intake progress, and understand where potential patients drop out of the process.

## The Solution

ReferralFlow brings referral management into a single interactive dashboard, giving practice administrators and intake coordinators a clearer view of their operations.

### Current Prototype Features

- **Dashboard:** Referral counts, pipeline stages, and operational metrics.
- **Referral Management:** Search, filter, and review referral records.
- **Follow-Up Tracking:** Identify referrals requiring attention.
- **Waitlist Visibility:** View and manage waitlisted referrals.
- **Referral Details:** Explore individual records through an interactive interface.
- **Reports:** Demonstration views of operational reporting.
- **Import Interface:** Preview of a future data-import workflow.

## Long-Term Vision

ReferralFlow is intended to evolve beyond referral tracking into a business intelligence platform for therapy practices.

Potential future capabilities include:

- Referral-to-intake conversion analytics
- Revenue opportunity tracking
- Patient retention reporting
- Provider utilization metrics
- Referral source performance
- Operational forecasting

## Technology

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

The prototype was developed using AI-assisted coding tools, with product direction, requirements, and workflow design driven by the project creator.

## Project Status

**Interactive proof of concept — not a production application.**

The current version uses fictional referral data and runs entirely in the browser.

It does not include a backend, database, authentication, or persistent data storage. Certain actions, reports, and import workflows are simulated for demonstration purposes.

## Purpose

ReferralFlow is a product exploration focused on identifying a real operational problem, designing a practical solution, and translating business requirements into an interactive application.

The project demonstrates product thinking, workflow design, and rapid prototyping.
